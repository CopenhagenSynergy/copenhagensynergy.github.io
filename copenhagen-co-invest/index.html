The opportunity

Three strengths that never meet in one room

Local seed investors

Proprietary dealflow, founder trust, diligence on the ground.

Lack follow-on firepower.

Foreign investors

Capital, sector depth, global scaling networks.

Lack local sourcing & trust.

Public sector

Real innovation needs, testbeds, reference-customer credibility.

Lack a fast, compliant way to pull innovation in.

The idea

Co-invest in curated Copenhagen opportunities, alongside a committed local lead, at a table the City convenes and curates.

Copenhagen Capacity orchestrates the process — holding no capital, charging no deal fee, picking no winners. That neutrality is exactly what makes the introduction worth trusting.

Guardrails are the product

How it works

Identify → Develop → Place → Grow

01 Identify

Sector sprints

Cluster and ecosystem leaders surface 8–12 companies that already have a local investor and a real city-demand angle.

02 Develop

The table

CopCap convenes local lead, foreign co-investors and the public demand owner. Invited law & tax advisers run working sessions.

03 Place

The round

Investors structure it themselves — side-by-side, SPV, pledge or direct. CopCap gives process and templates, not terms.

04 Grow

Aftercare

Soft-landing, talent, entity setup and expansion — turning one deal into a lasting relationship.

The anchor

The City convenes and curates — it does not buy

  • Chairs the co-investment sessions, bringing curated companies and curated investors to one table.
  • Curates which companies take part — selected by sector and the city's genuine need for their innovation.
  • Signals demand and lends its standing; its commitment is to convene, not to purchase.
  • Any paid pilot that follows runs through a compliant process — innovation partnership, PCP, OPI or equal-terms testbed access.
A demand signal and a credibility marker that de-risks go-to-market — not a guaranteed contract.

Structuring

Transact fast, four ways

Side-by-side

Alongside the local lead, on the lead's terms. Simplest, lowest cost.

SPV / syndicate

Several foreign tickets under one cap-table line, run by a local admin.

Pledge / opt-in

Deal-by-deal discretion, no blind-pool commitment.

Direct

Large investors who want their own line.

CopCap maintains a vetted panel of Danish law, tax and fund-admin firms — introductions, not endorsements.

Trust

Neutral by design

  • A published, objective curation screen — local lead present, sector fit, verifiable facts.
  • A conflict register; no CopCap board seats, no carry.
  • Information parity — every investor at a table gets the same verified pack.
  • A documented no-fee-on-capital rule.
  • A legal gate — procurement & state-aid sign-off on every public demand instrument.

Roadmap

Phase 3 · Platform

18 months +

Standing syndicates, routine cross-border flows, the aftercare retention loop closing.
