Who it is for

Three kinds of participant, one route in

Each participant gets a passport: a personal route through Denmark's ecosystems. Every stage adds a stamp: new knowledge, new contacts, a step closer to a decision.

International startups

Founders weighing Denmark as a first market or base in Europe.

  • First customers and pilot partners
  • Incubators, accelerators and research partners
  • Investors active in their field

Mature businesses

Established companies considering a Danish office, R&D site or regional hub.

  • Industry partners, suppliers and customers
  • Universities and technology institutes
  • Municipalities and public agencies

Investors

Funds, family offices and corporate investors looking at Danish technology.

  • Companies in their investment focus
  • Danish co-investors and funds
  • Clusters that show where activity is

The programme

Four stages, from first look to first office

Stages 1 and 2 run online, so participants arrive in Denmark knowing who they will meet and why. Stage 3 is the visit. Stage 4 turns the decision into practical steps.

  1. STAGE01

    Explore

    Online

    Onboarding and a guided introduction to how Denmark's innovation ecosystems are organised.

    • Participant profile: what you do, what you want to achieve in Denmark, who you want to meet
    • Online sessions on the national innovation clusters, research environments and capital landscape
    • A first look at the practical routes: company set-up, work permits and talent
    Stamp earnedA completed profile, ready for matchmaking.
  2. STAGE02

    Connect

    Online · matched introductions

    The profile is matched against the members of Danish ecosystems in the Danish Ecosystem Atlas. A CopCap specialist checks every suggestion before anyone is contacted.

    • A shortlist of relevant organisations, each with the reason it was suggested
    • Introductions only where both sides agree
    • First online conversations before anyone travels
    Stamp earnedAgreed introductions and a meeting plan for the visit.
  3. STAGE03

    Visit

    In person · Greater Copenhagen and Denmark

    A tailor-made visit programme built around the introductions from Stage 2.

    • Meetings with matched companies, investors, researchers and public partners
    • Visits to clusters, innovation hubs and test facilities
    • Business events in the Eastern Denmark events calendar, where the timing fits
    Stamp earnedA first-hand view of the ecosystem and a network on the ground.
  4. STAGE04

    Establish

    Practical steps · with Copenhagen Capacity

    For participants ready to move: CopCap's set-up services, from location choice to the first hires.

    • Location benchmarking and set-up advice
    • Finance introductions and the sustainability network
    • Talent attraction and recruitment support
    • Continued support after the company has established
    Stamp earnedA plan for establishing or expanding in Denmark, or a well-informed "not yet".

Matchmaking before introductions

Linked to the Danish Ecosystem Atlas

Before any introduction, each participant is matched against the members of Danish ecosystems recorded in the Danish Ecosystem Atlas. Stage 2 therefore starts from a reasoned shortlist rather than from a general network.

  1. Profile

    What the participant does, their value-chain position, preferred location, and the kinds of organisation they want to meet.

  2. Match

    The Atlas ranks ecosystem members and shows why each one fits: shared focus words, the same value-chain stage, location.

  3. Review

    A CopCap specialist checks every suggestion, removes poor fits and adds context the data cannot show.

  4. Introduce

    Introductions are made only when both sides agree, online first and then during the visit.

Coverage today. The Atlas currently holds one ecosystem: the 502 members of Energy Cluster Denmark, taken from the cluster's own member list. Location, stakeholder type and value-chain stage are added automatically and may need review. More of Denmark's national clusters will be added over time.

Confidentiality. Participant profiles are never published on this site. The matching in the Atlas runs in the browser and saves nothing. Copenhagen Capacity's services are confidential.

The ecosystems

Denmark's national innovation clusters

Denmark has 11 national innovation clusters for 2025–2028. Ten of them have their headquarters or an office in Greater Copenhagen; Odense Robotics is the exception. The Passport draws on them to find the right people in each field.
